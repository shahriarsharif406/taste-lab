# Demo video script (about 2:30, record in English)

**0:00 Hook (15s).** "Small brands selling to the US and EU can't afford focus groups. ChatGPT personas are guesses. Taste Lab grounds them in Qloo's taste graph."

**0:15 Input (15s).** Click *Use example product* (Ridgeline Daypack). Mention you can also paste a product-page URL.

**0:30 Run (20s).** Press *Run Taste Lab*. Narrate the live status: designing personas, querying Qloo per persona, focus group, city ranking, ad copy.

**0:50 Personas and evidence (35s).** Open one persona. Point at the "Qloo artist / brand / movie signal" lines and an evidence id like `Q7`. Open the *Qloo evidence & trace* tab: "Every claim maps to a real Qloo entity, affinity score and the exact API call."

**1:25 Focus group (20s).** Show intent scores, loves/concerns and the moderator synthesis: "This tells me who to target first and what objection to answer."

**1:45 City fit (20s).** Show the bar chart (Austin, Berlin, Amsterdam...) and the top Qloo places behind each score.

**2:05 Ad copy (15s).** Show persona-specific headlines and "why this hook" tied to a Qloo signal.

**2:20 Close (10s).** Download the HTML report. "Open source, MIT. Taste Lab: know your buyers before you spend on ads."

Recording checklist: key configured so the DEMO banner is NOT shown; example product loaded; browser zoom 110%; no secrets visible on screen.
